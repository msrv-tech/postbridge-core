# Meta platform marks

These local marks identify saved Facebook and Instagram connections. They were
obtained from Meta's official primary sources on 2026-11-13. They are Meta
trademarks/copyrighted brand assets, used subject to the linked brand guidelines;
they are not covered by an open-source license granted by this repository.

## Facebook

Source and guidelines: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/

`facebook.svg` is the complete inline `Facebook app logo` SVG from that official
page, with the SVG XML namespace added for standalone use and inherited
`currentColor` resolved to Facebook Blue `#0866ff`. The official download's primary
PNG confirms that color. All original paths and coordinates are unchanged. The
transparent letter sits over the UI's white matte, preserving the primary mark.

## Rendering and inspection

Keep the geometry, colors and aspect ratios intact. Both marks are shown at
32 by 32 CSS pixels with `object-fit: contain` and 12 pixels of clear space.
The SVGs were parsed and inspected: no scripts, event handlers, linked resources,
embedded raster images, external references, fonts, or URL-based styles. No new
asset package or runtime dependency is used. Source captures and downloads are
retained in the review artifacts under `meta-ui-owner-inventory-20261113/brand-input`.
